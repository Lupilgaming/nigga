🔍 1. What is the Screener & What Is Its Exact Impact on Backtests?

A common trap in quantitative finance is assuming every stock in an expanded thematic universe has the same statistical distribution. Mean-reversion indicators (like Bollinger Bands) mathematically require mean-reverting, stationary oscillations. When applied blindly to stocks experiencing structural collapse or parabolic speculative bubbles, the math breaks down. The GrowGarden Multi-Stage Screener acts as an intelligent gatekeeper, running four quantitative filters before permitting any capital allocation:

Gate 1: Asset Type & Strategy Routing

Runaway momentum assets (e.g., HUDCO, MAZDOCK, DATAPATTNS, DIXON) violate Gaussian distribution assumptions. When they correct, they drop 15–25% before recovering. The screener detects ADX > 32 and re-routes these assets to Intraday Squeeze Momentum / Breakout instead of fading them on Bollinger Bands.

Gate 2: Macro Structural Trend (`Close > 200 EMA`)

Only stocks operating in a confirmed Stage 2 Structural Uptrend are eligible. Stocks trading below their 200-day EMA are in institutional distribution; lower-band touches in downtrends are traps, not value dips.

Gate 3: Quality & Sector Sentiment Health

Requires Sector Sentiment Score ≥ 7.5 or Capital Efficiency (ROCE ≥ 12.0%). This guarantees that oversold pullbacks represent institutional dip accumulation rather than corporate deterioration.

Gate 4: Execution Bar Confirmation (`Close > Open`)

The strategy never blindly places limit orders at the band. It requires an active bullish reversal candle (green daily bar closing higher than open inside the band) confirming buyers have stepped in.

⚠️ 2. Inherent Strategy Flaws & Failure Modes (Full Quantitative Transparency)

To deploy capital prudently, a quant must know exactly where an algorithm fails. Triangular Bollinger Bands (15 TRIMA, 1.5 STD) + RSI have four distinct vulnerabilities:

Flaw 1: Falling Knives in Stage 4 Downtrends

When an equity enters secular institutional distribution (e.g. IDEA, YESBANK, RPOWER), the lower band continuously drags downward. Fading each lower-band touch causes repeated stop-outs. Mitigation: Strictly eliminated by Gate 2 (Close > 200 EMA).

Flaw 2: The Runaway Momentum Paradox

In explosive multi-baggers (e.g. HUDCO, MAZDOCK), normal pullbacks are high-velocity shakes (-15% to -25%). The lower Bollinger Band fails to act as support because price punches far below 1.5 std dev. Mitigation: Re-routed by Gate 1 to Squeeze Momentum.

Flaw 3: Profit Capping on Multi-Baggers

Bollinger mean reversion exits at the upper band or when dynamic RSI reaches overbought (+5% to +10% target). On a stock that doubles or quadruples, the strategy prematurely clips winners, leaving secular multi-bagger gains on the table.

Flaw 4: Stagnation & Friction Drag in Dead Zones

In low-volatility, flat-lining consolidation stocks, prices drift sideways for weeks without reaching the upper band. Positions get closed by time-stop rules while eating exchange transaction fees and brokerage friction.
