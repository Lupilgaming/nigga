✅ Why 15:10 IST Square-Off is Protective

1. Zero Broker RMS Penalty Fees: Zerodha and Groww levy a mandatory ₹50.00 + 18% GST (= ₹59.00 per order) call-and-trade penalty fee if their automated RMS engine liquidates the position between 15:15 and 15:20 IST.
2. Zero Queue Slippage on Losers: When a stock breaks down (e.g. NMDC dropping -2.0% into close), thousands of retail market sell orders swamp the bid simultaneously. 15:10 exits front-run the broker liquidation cascade.
3. Zero Server Latency Traps: At 15:15:00 IST, broker order gateways experience peak concurrent execution traffic, occasionally delaying order acknowledgements by 2 to 6 seconds.

⚠️ The Opportunity Cost on Strong Momentum Leaders

1. Short-Covering Drift: High-beta winners in strong secular uptrends (like HUDCO or PFC) experience aggressive buy-to-close orders from intraday short-sellers between 15:15 and 15:25 IST.
2. Closing Auction Matching: Closing prices on NSE are computed as the 30-minute VWAP (15:00 – 15:30). Institutional algorithms targeting closing benchmark prices steadily absorb ask depth, propelling price higher into 15:30.
3. Fixed-Time Inefficiency: A rigid, blind clock exit at 15:10 treats winners and losers identically, prematurely aborting trades that still possess positive order book momentum.

🎯 Production Architectural Solution: The Dynamic OBI Exit Protocol

Instead of a rigid binary choice (15:10 vs 15:25), our quantitative execution engine introduces the Dynamic Order Book Imbalance (OBI) Exit Protocol:

Rule A: Positive Imbalance (OBI ≥ +30%) at 15:10 IST

If Top-5 Order Book Imbalance remains heavily buy-dominant (OBI ≥ +0.30), the strategy moves its stop-loss up to a guaranteed breakeven floor (+0.15R) and allows the position to ride the short-covering wave until 15:22 IST. This captures the +0.6% to +0.8% late-session drift while keeping profit 100% safeguarded.

Rule B: Neutral or Negative Imbalance (OBI ≤ +10%) at 15:10 IST

If Top-5 Order Book Imbalance deteriorates or flips negative (OBI ≤ +0.10), the position is liquidated immediately at 15:10 IST via limit cross. This completely avoids the broker RMS liquidation cascade (15:15–15:20) and saves the ₹59 broker penalty fee.
